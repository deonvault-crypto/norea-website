Privacy Policy

Noréa collects only the information needed to operate a physical-goods ecommerce service: name, email, phone number, delivery address, order details and support messages.

We do not request contacts, SMS logs, call logs, background location or tracking permissions. Payment card details are handled by secure payment providers and are not stored by Noréa.

How Data Is Used

Customer data is used to process orders, verify payments, coordinate delivery, provide support, prevent fraud and maintain required transaction records.

Notifications

Push notifications are optional and may include order, delivery, payment and restock updates. Customers can disable notifications in device settings.

Account Deletion

Customers may initiate account deletion from the Noréa app settings or contact support. Some order records may be retained when required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention or dispute resolution.

Contact

For privacy requests, visit Noréa Support.
