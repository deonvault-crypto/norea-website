Terms of Service

Noréa sells physical activewear and athleisure products. The app does not sell digital goods, coins, subscriptions, gambling products or premium digital unlocks.

Orders and Payments

Checkout supports approved online payment methods for physical goods, including Paynow Zimbabwe, supported mobile money, card and Zimbabwe bank transfer options. Orders are confirmed after payment verification.

Delivery

Nation-wide delivery in Zimbabwe is estimated at 6-10 days after payment confirmation, subject to stock, address accuracy and courier availability.

Returns and Refunds

Refund and return requests are reviewed for eligible unworn items in original packaging. Payment reversals may depend on provider and bank processing timelines.

Support

For sizing, checkout, delivery, refund or account questions, visit Noréa Support.
