Support

Noréa support helps with sizing, checkout, delivery, payments, refunds and account requests.

Orders and Delivery

Use your order number when contacting support. Nation-wide delivery in Zimbabwe is estimated at 6-10 days after payment confirmation.

Refunds and Returns

Refund and return requests are reviewed for unworn items in original packaging and may depend on provider processing timelines.

Account and Privacy Requests

Customers can initiate account deletion inside the Noréa app settings or contact support for privacy assistance.
